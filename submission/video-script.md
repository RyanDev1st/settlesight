# SettleSight — Demo Video Script & Storyboard

- **Target Video Length:** 2 minutes 45 seconds (under the 2:50 limit)
- **Format:** High-definition 1080p screen-recording only (no camera face recording, no third-party audio)
- **Pacing:** ~150 words per minute (Total spoken words: ~410 words across 165 seconds)
- **Narrator Voice:** Clear, professional English voiceover (live human or high-clarity synthetic TTS)

## Script Production Checklist for Video Recorder

1. **Resolution & Scaling:** Set screen resolution to 1920x1080 at 100% display scale.
2. **Clean Browser Window:** Open Chrome or Edge in full screen (press F11 or borderless window), hide bookmarks bar and extensions.
3. **Audio Quality:** Record voiceover with clean condenser microphone or high-quality neural voiceover at 150 words per minute.
4. **Mouse Smoothing:** Move mouse deliberately between widgets without jittery cursor movements.
5. **Final Video Upload:** Export as MP4 (H.264, AAC), upload to YouTube as Unlisted/Public, and verify duration is 2:45 (< 3:00).
